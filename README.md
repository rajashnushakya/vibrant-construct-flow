# Vivid Vision Builders

Project Title: Interactive & Minimalist Website for [Company Name]




1. Project Overview & Objective

We need a modern, visually striking, yet simple website to showcase our construction and design services. The design must feel clean and uncluttered, relying on smooth, subtle animations rather than heavy text to guide visitors through our offerings.




2. Brand Identity & Color Palette

The overall design must seamlessly incorporate our brand identity using these exact primary brand colors:




Deep Blue: #004B9C (Primary headers, main CTA buttons, dynamic accents)

Vibrant Green: #039E4D (Hover effects, success badges, subtle animated highlights)

Neutral Backgrounds: Crisp off-white (#F8F9FA) and charcoal (#1A1A1A) for high contrast and readability.

3. Website Structure & Key Pages




Hero Section (Homepage):




Minimalist headline summarizing our core vision.

Full-screen dynamic visual or subtle background video loop of completed projects.

Single interactive CTA button: "Explore Our Work".

Services Page / Interactive Section:




Residential Construction: Custom home builds and turn-key modern housing.

Commercial Projects: Office spaces, retail hubs, and commercial builds.

Interior Design: Space planning, luxury finishings, and modern aesthetic styling.

Renovation & Upgrades: Modernizing older homes and complete structural revamps.

Interaction: Sleek tabbed or card-swipe navigation with micro-animations when hovering or switching services.

Portfolio / Projects Gallery:




Filterable gallery (All, Residential, Commercial, Renovation, Interiors).

An interactive slider showing "Before & After" transformations for house upgrades.

About Us:




Brief overview of our quality craftsmanship and commitment to excellence.

Contact & Estimation Inquiry:




Simple multi-step contact form for project inquiries.

Direct WhatsApp / Phone quick-action button.

4. Animation & UX Requirements




Smooth Scroll: Seamless vertical transition between sections.

Micro-Interactions: Subtle scale and color transitions on buttons when hovering.

Scroll-Triggered Reveals: Content elements and project images fade or slide in gently as the user scrolls.

Performance First: Animations must be lightweight (e.g., Framer Motion or GSAP) so the site loads instantly on mobile and desktop. now make a website with theme and localization(mean to say language can be changed to nepali and english both with toggle.
